Research

We conduct integrated experimental and numerical research toward low-damage, high-performance, hazard-resilient structural systems. We aim to reduce the sensitivity of structural response to the variability of earthquake ground motions, and we work with industry partners to strengthen the two-way transfer of knowledge between practice and research.

Projects

Seismic design of precast reinforced concrete arch bridges
2026–2029 California Department of Transportation (Caltrans)
Design criteria and performance evaluation for prefabricated reinforced concrete arches, combining soil–structure interaction analyses with material, component and system tests.
Shake table testing of a 230 kV power transformer
2026–2027 WEGAI Research and Idaho National Laboratory; US Department of Energy
Triaxial shake-table tests of a 230 kV transformer with and without seismic response modification devices and spring washer uniaxial tests.
Resilience-based design of buildings with accelerated post-earthquake functional recovery
2024–2027 NIST–NSF Disaster Resilience Research Grant Program
Resilience-based design of buildings with reusable force-limiting connections that reduce damage and accelerate post-earthquake functional recovery.
Performance-based seismic design of cut-and-cover tunnels
2023–2026 California Department of Transportation (Caltrans)
Large-scale racking tests of reinforced concrete tunnel specimens and calibrated nonlinear models to develop performance-based design criteria.
Suspended-floor building systems
2024–present UC San Diego
Historical overview, state of the art and seismic response of buildings whose floors hang from a core or transfer structure.
Uniform flexural yielding structural fuse connections for mass timber braced frames
2026–2027 Fulbright Scholar Program (U.S. Department of State and Pakistan Fulbright Commission); UC San Diego
A replaceable steel fuse connection for mass timber braced frames, made from machined steel angles that yield uniformly in flexure, characterized through quasi-static cyclic tests, digital image correlation and finite element models.
Connection with predetermined discrete variable friction forces (Modified Friction Device)
2021–2026 UC San Diego
A structural connection that develops predetermined, discrete friction forces at target displacements, from design and finite element simulation to a full-scale brace test.
Low-cost friction connection with reduced sensitivity to machining tolerances and accelerated repairability
2023–2026 UC San Diego
A low-cost friction-based force-limiting connection whose friction force is less sensitive to machining tolerances and that can be repaired quickly after an earthquake.
Composite friction materials: mechanical and tribological characterization
2022–2025 Precast/Prestressed Concrete Institute (Daniel P. Jenny Fellowship); UC San Diego
Experimental characterization of the mechanical and tribological properties of composite materials for friction-based force-limiting structural components.
Full-scale shake-table tests of steel buildings with sliding slabs
2023–2025 Collaboration with NCREE and National Taiwan University (Chung-Che Chou), UC San Diego (Chia-Ming Uang) and UT Arlington (Shih-Ho Chao)
Full-scale shake-table tests at NCREE of three-story steel buildings with sliding slabs connected through energy-dissipating devices.
Seismic design provisions for diaphragms and higher-mode responses from instrumented buildings
2022–2024 California Strong Motion Instrumentation Program (CSMIP)
Strong-motion data from instrumented California buildings used to validate diaphragm design provisions and assess higher-mode responses.
Core wall buildings with force-limiting connections
2021–2024 UC San Diego
Force-limiting floor-to-wall connections that reduce higher-mode shear, torsion and acceleration responses in tall core wall buildings.
Edge distance effects in OSB sheathing-to-framing nailed connections
2023–2025 Mulhern + Kulp Structural Engineering
Experimental characterization of the edge distance effects on the uplift response of OSB sheathing-to-framing nailed connections using digital image correlation.
Force-based design method for force-limiting deformable connections
2016–2022 Lehigh University; ATLSS Engineering Research Center
A force-based method to design force-limiting deformable connections in cast-in-place reinforced concrete walls, unbonded post-tensioned rocking walls, and steel braced frames with controlled rocking base mechanism.
NEESR: Inertial force-limiting floor anchorage systems
2011–2016 National Science Foundation (CMMI-1135033, NEES)
Deformable floor anchorage connections with friction devices or buckling-restrained braces, from full-scale component tests to a shake-table test.
Performance-based strength reduction factors for steel moment-resisting frames
2009–2011 University of Patras (MSc thesis)
A parametric study of the inelastic response of planar steel moment-resisting frames to estimate the Eurocode 8 behavior factor from a target performance level.
